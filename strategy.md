# Investment Goal

Buy and hold rental properties.

## Priority

- Stable neighborhoods
- Good appreciation potential
- Low maintenance

## Avoid

- Flood zones
- Major renovations
- High crime areas

## Prefer

- 3 bedrooms
- 1000–1800 sqft
- Commuting distance to NYC
- Climate resilient

## Scoring philosophy

- Better neighborhood > higher yield
- Lower maintenance > slightly better return
- Long-term appreciation > short-term speculation

## Category score semantics (locked)

All category scores use the same direction:

- **0** = poor match / high concern
- **10** = strong match / low concern

**Risk** means risk *quality*: **10 = low risk**, **0 = high risk**.

This file changes only when investment philosophy or weights change.
