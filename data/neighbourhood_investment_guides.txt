Weinland Park
Rental: Very strong. Excellent proximity to OSU, Short North and Downtown creates a large pool of students, young professionals and university-related renters. Duplexes and small multifamily properties can produce strong rents relative to acquisition prices, although tenant turnover can be high.
Appreciation: Very strong. Central location, continued redevelopment and still-relatively-low prices give it substantial upside. I would expect appreciation to be more street-specific here than in Bexley or Grandview.
Flood: Generally low to moderate, but individual parcels need checking for drainage and proximity to waterways.
Future improvements: Continued central-city redevelopment, housing investment and zoning changes should support density. Its proximity to OSU, Short North and Downtown is itself a major long-term catalyst.
Investment view: One of the strongest risk/reward neighborhoods on your list.

Italian Village
Rental: Excellent. One of the deepest and highest-quality rental markets in Columbus because of Short North, OSU, Downtown and the young professional population.
Appreciation: Excellent. Historic housing, scarce land and proximity to major employment/entertainment centers make this one of the safest appreciation bets.
Flood: Generally low, with parcel-level drainage still worth checking.
Future improvements: Benefits from continued Short North/Downtown investment, central-city infill and zoning changes. The neighborhood doesn't need a future transit catalyst as much as outer neighborhoods because it already has exceptional access.
Investment view: Excellent, but finding a legal 2–4 unit at your price is the challenge.

Victorian Village
Rental: Excellent. OSU, Short North, Goodale Park and Downtown create very strong demand.
Appreciation: Excellent. Historic character and central location should continue supporting values.
Flood: Generally low to moderate depending on exact location.
Future improvements: Particularly interesting because the Northwest BRT corridor connects Downtown, the Arena District, Grandview and OSU. The first phase is progressing through design. linkuscolumbus.com
Investment view: Excellent fundamentals, but likely too expensive for the yield you want unless you find an unusual deal.

Old North
Rental: Excellent, especially because of OSU and the student/young-professional market.
Appreciation: Very good. Strong location and persistent rental demand support long-term values.
Flood: Generally low to moderate. Check parcels near drainage corridors.
Future improvements: Benefits from Northwest BRT and broader OSU/central Columbus investment.
Investment view: Strong rental play, but expect more management intensity than Bexley.

Olde Towne East
Rental: Very good. Strong demand from young professionals and residents wanting proximity to Downtown.
Appreciation: Very good to excellent. Historic housing and proximity to Downtown give it substantial upside.
Flood: Generally low to moderate.
Future improvements: Continued Near East Side redevelopment, urban infill and improved transit connectivity.
Investment view: One of the best central neighborhoods for finding value in small multifamily.

Merion Village
Rental: Very good. Strong demand from young professionals and families, with German Village/Downtown nearby.
Appreciation: Very good. It gets some of the desirability spillover from German Village while remaining more affordable.
Flood: Moderate in some locations, particularly toward the river/drainage system.
Future improvements: Continued South Side redevelopment and urban infill. Less of a specific BRT play than Bexley or Hilltop.
Investment view: Strong all-around neighborhood if the numbers work.

Bexley
Rental: Very good. Strong tenant pool, good schools, Capital University, Main Street and proximity to Downtown.
Appreciation: Excellent. This is one of the strongest appreciation locations in your entire list.
Flood: Generally low, but check individual properties.
Future improvements: Major transit catalyst. East Main BRT will run through Downtown, Bexley, Whitehall and Reynoldsburg, with 17 stations and dedicated infrastructure. The project is nearing 90% design. linkuscolumbus.com
Investment view: One of my favorites for you. Your 920 Ferndale duplex is a good example of why.

Upper Arlington
Rental: Very good, although high property prices can compress yields.
Appreciation: Excellent. Strong schools, affluent demographics and established desirability.
Flood: Mostly low to moderate, with more attention needed toward the Olentangy.
Future improvements: Northwest BRT is the major long-term catalyst. Line 30 now provides a direct Hilliard → Upper Arlington → OSU → Downtown connection every 30 minutes, seven days a week. prodwww.cota.com
Investment view: Excellent appreciation, but usually not enough cash flow at $300k.

New Albany

Flood: Generally low, although Big Walnut/Blacklick drainage areas need parcel-level checking.
Future improvements: Continued northeast employment and infrastructure growth. Less of a direct BRT story.
Investment view: Great appreciation suburb, but not one of my first choices for a $300k duplex.

Future improvements: Benefits from East Main corridor investment and Bexley/east-side spillover.
